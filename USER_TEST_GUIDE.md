# Trying the ED Cost Estimator (10 to 15 minutes)

Thank you for helping. This is a class project: an app that estimates what an emergency department visit will cost a patient, using Yale New Haven Hospital's published prices.

## Before you start

- Do not type anything that identifies a real patient. Describe made-up or generic cases ("45-year-old with chest pain for an hour").
- The app estimates the hospital's bill only. The physician's bill is separate.

## What to do

1. Open the link you were sent.
2. Try **three cases you know well**, typed the way a patient would say them. For example: an ankle injury, chest pain, a cut that needs stitches, belly pain, a kidney stone, a headache.
3. For at least one, type an insurer (for example "Aetna" or "no insurance"). If you like, open **I know my plan details** and enter a copay, deductible and coinsurance.
4. For each case, look at three things on the page: the **visit level**, the **expected and possible tests**, and the **most-likely dollar figure**.

## Six questions

Please send your answers back by text or email. A sentence or two each is plenty.

1. Your role (for example "ED attending", "ED nurse", "PA"). No name is needed.
2. Which cases did you try?
3. Were the visit level and the expected tests what you would have predicted? What was wrong or missing?
4. Today, if a patient asks you "what will this cost me?", what do you say, and how long would it take you to find a real answer?
5. Would this be useful to you or your patients? Why or why not? (One sentence I can quote would be great.)
6. Any other tips or recommendations would be helpful.

If you can, also send **one screenshot** of a case you ran.
